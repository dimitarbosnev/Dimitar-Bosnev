Hello!

I'm Dimitar Bosnev

C/C++ Software Engineer

I'm a software engineer with 2+ years of professional C/C++ experience, currently building digital twin tooling at AVES Reality. I specialize in high-performance 3D rendering and game engine tooling, and I work with large-scale geospatial data, such as terrain and GIS formats, in real-time applications.

My path started with a childhood fascination with video games and a desire to understand how they work, which led me to study Creative Media and Game Technologies at Saxion. I care about robust, well-engineered solutions that never compromise on performance or quality.

Whether it's engines, rendering, or a potential collaboration, feel free to reach out.
